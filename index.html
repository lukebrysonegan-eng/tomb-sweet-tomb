King Naptep wants to sleep

You are a 3,000-year-old mummy. An endless stream of tiny tomb raiders is running and jumping down your pyramid to carry off your golden jars. One touch from any trap finishes a raider.

  1. Pick a trap from the shop and tap a corridor tile to build it.
  2. Open the gates. The stream never stops and keeps getting thicker.
  3. Keep building while they run. Tap a built trap to upgrade or sell it.
