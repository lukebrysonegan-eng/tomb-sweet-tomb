The scouts are learning

You command a supply depot at the end of a quiet field. Each generation of scouts copies the moves of the ones that got furthest, so they learn to jump the ponds and then your defences. Everything you have unlocked is free to build and move. Coins come only from holding: every 15 seconds with no scout reaching the depot pays out. Spend them on unlocking weapons, upgrading them and extending the field.
